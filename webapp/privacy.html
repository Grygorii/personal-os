📕 Kept

Privacy

Last updated 1 August 2026 · Kept is run by one person in Ireland.

Plain version: Kept stores your books, the thoughts you write about them, your conversations with the mentor, and your exam results. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the two services below that are needed to make it work.

What is stored

  • Your account — the name and email address Google gives us when you sign in, and a Telegram id if you connect one.
  • Your library — books, reading progress, ratings, and the thoughts you save against a page.
  • Your conversations with the mentor, so it can remember what you've discussed.
  • Your exams — the questions, your answers, and the grade.
  • Basic usage counts — messages and exams per day, used only to enforce daily limits.
  • Messages you send us — if you write from the menu, the message is kept so it can be read and answered, along with which screen you were on when you wrote it.

No passwords are stored: Google handles sign-in. No payment details are held — there is nothing to pay for yet.

What is public — and only what you choose

Nothing in your library is public by default. A page at /r/… is created only when you tap Share, and it contains only what you ticked on that screen: the book, the thoughts you selected, and your score if you included it. Anyone with the link can read it. Ask and it will be deleted.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic and/or Google (Gemini) — the AI models. Your messages and book details are sent to them to generate replies, exams and recommendations.
  • MongoDB Atlas (database) and Railway (hosting) store and run it.

Messages you send us are not sent to any AI model. They are read by one person.

That's the whole list. There are no third-party analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no third-party cookies. Three cookies exist: the one that keeps you signed in; if you arrived from a shared link, a short-lived one remembering which link it was; and, only if you write to us without an account, one that lets you come back and read the reply. That last one is set at the moment you send a message and never for simply reading.

One number is kept: how many times each page was loaded on each day. It's a counter and nothing else — no cookie, no IP address, no identifier, no record of which pages you personally opened. It exists only to answer "did anyone visit today", and no visitor can be reconstructed from it.

Your rights

You can get everything held about you, or have all of it deleted. Both are real buttons, not a promise: send /export or /delete-me to the bot, or email the address below and it will be done by hand. Deletion removes your account and every record attached to it.
