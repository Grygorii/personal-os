# The blind-spot ladder — A1 → C2

The spine of your curriculum. Not a syllabus to *learn* top-to-bottom — you're
already fluent — but a **diagnostic map**. We sweep each level, confirm what's solid,
and surface the silent gaps immersion left behind. Immersion builds a *jagged*
profile: C1 in some places, A2 in others. This map finds the low rungs so we fix them
before they trip you (like "holistic" did).

**Status:** ✓ solid · ~ shaky · ✗ confirmed gap · ? not tested yet

We test cold, mark honestly, and a rung isn't "done" until its gaps are ✓. The
Cambridge exams sit at the level checkpoints — we don't chase them; we earn them by
clearing the rungs beneath.

## Every rung has the same four checks

At each level we look through **all of these**:
1. **Grammar & structure** — the rules that hold sentences together.
2. **Vocabulary & word-understanding** — knowing words *precisely*, all their senses.
3. **Pronunciation** — saying words right: stress, silent letters, homophones. A
   mispronounced word makes a fluent speaker sound unsure. See `rules/04-pronunciation.md`.
4. **Register — sounding professional** — turning correct English into the polished,
   professional English a Team Lead should write and speak. *(I also correct how you
   phrase things in real time, not just in drills.)*

Why four, not just grammar: your goal is to be the person companies chase and, when
you choose, a founder. Each of these is a **signal of level** — they make you *read*
as someone in command, which is the real point.
