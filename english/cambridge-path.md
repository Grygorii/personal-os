# The Cambridge path — your route to a C2 certificate

The plan for turning "I speak good English" into **proof on your LinkedIn**:
a real Cambridge certificate, backed by real knowledge and real word-precision.

## The ladder — one exam per level

Cambridge English has one qualification for each CEFR level. They renamed them
in 2019; the old names still appear everywhere, so both are here.

| CEFR | Current name    | Old name | Meaning              |
| ---- | --------------- | -------- | -------------------- |
| A2   | A2 Key          | KET      | basic                |
| B1   | B1 Preliminary  | PET      | intermediate         |
| B2   | B2 First        | FCE      | upper-intermediate   |
| C1   | C1 Advanced     | CAE      | advanced             |
| **C2** | **C2 Proficiency** | **CPE** | **mastery — the top** |

**C2 Proficiency (CPE) is the exam that certifies C2.** That is the summit.

## Two facts that matter for LinkedIn

1. **Cambridge certificates never expire.** Your result is valid *indefinitely* — it
   proves that on that date you performed at that level, for life. (Individual
   universities/employers may choose to only accept certificates from the last 2–3
   years, but for a profile credential it stands forever.) IELTS, by contrast, is
   effectively treated as valid ~2 years — a real reason to prefer Cambridge here.
2. **Built-in safety net.** In C2 Proficiency, a score of **200–230 → a C2
   certificate**. Fall a little short (**180–199**) and you **still receive a C1
   certificate**. You never walk away with nothing.

## What the exam actually contains (and where "words" live)

C2 Proficiency is **~4 hours**, five equally-weighted parts. Your final score is the
average of all five on the **Cambridge English Scale (200–230 = C2)**.

| Part | Time | What it tests | Your "three sides" |
| ---- | ---- | ------------- | ------------------ |
| **Reading & Use of English** | ~1h30 | reading + **vocabulary, word-formation, precision** | ② knowledge + **③ words** |
| **Writing**  | ~1h30 | an essay + one other task (register control) | ② knowledge |
| **Listening** | ~40 min | four recordings | ② knowledge |
| **Speaking** | ~16 min | interview + collaborative task (with a partner) | ② knowledge |

**The key insight:** your obsession with *understanding words* is not a side quest —
**"Use of English" is a whole graded paper**, worth a fifth of your C2 score. The
study deck (`study.html`) is direct training for it.

## The three sides, stated plainly

- **① Certificate** — the credential you put on LinkedIn. Earned by passing the exam.
- **② Language knowledge** — you can *perform*: read, write, listen, speak at C2.
- **③ Understanding of words** — deep, precise vocabulary. The thing that made
  "holistic" derail you. Trained here every class; tested as Use of English.

A certificate without ②/③ is hollow; ②/③ without the certificate is unprovable.
We build all three together.

## LinkedIn: how to list it (when earned)

- Add the certificate under **Licenses & Certifications** — Cambridge issues a
  verifiable certificate employers can check online.
- In the **Languages** section, set English to **"Full professional proficiency"**
  (the honest match for C2; reserve "Native or bilingual" for mother tongue).
- A C1 Advanced pass already justifies **"Professional working proficiency"** — so a
  milestone cert is postable *before* you reach the summit.

## The route: climb the rungs, exams are checkpoints

We do **not** chase a distant certificate. The plan is the blind-spot ladder
(`blind-spots.md`): start at the bottom, sweep each level A → C2, fix every gap across
grammar, words, and professional register. The Cambridge exams sit at the level
checkpoints and we **earn** them as we clear the rungs:

| When you're solid at… | The checkpoint you can take |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- |
| B2 | **B2 First (FCE)** — a real certificate, posts as "Professional working" |
| C1 | **C1 Advanced (CAE)** — "Full professional"; a high score already reports C2 |
| C2 | **C2 Proficiency (CPE)** — the summit; the definitive C2 credential |

You don't have to decide the finish line now. We climb; when a rung is genuinely
solid, its exam becomes a natural, low-risk checkpoint — and a real thing to put on
LinkedIn along the way. The next gap is always the focus.
