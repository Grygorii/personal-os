# Pronunciation — sounding sure, not just correct

A fluent speaker who mispronounces a word suddenly sounds unsure — and for your goal
(being the person companies chase), *sounding* in command matters as much as being
right. So pronunciation is a real aspect of every word we learn, not an extra.

## How I write sounds (no strange symbols)

We don't use IPA — those symbols would just be more things you don't know. Instead,
**plain respelling with the stressed part in CAPITALS**:

- *nuance* → **NEW-ahnss** · *subtle* → **SUT-ul** · *holistic* → **hoh-LISS-tik**

Say the CAPITAL part a touch louder and longer. English word-stress is real: put it on
the wrong part and the word can become hard to recognise.

In the study deck, every word shows its respelling and a **Hear it** button that says
it aloud. Use both: read the respelling, then hear it, then say it yourself.

## Silent letters — the traps

Whole letters you write but do **not** say. The classic ones:

| Silent | Examples |
| ------ | -------- |
| **b** | su**b**tle, dou**b**t, de**b**t, com**b**, thum**b** |
| **k** | **k**now, **k**nee, **k**nife, **k**nock |
| **w** | **w**rite, **w**rong, **w**hole, s**w**ord, ans**w**er |
| **gh** | thorou**gh**, thou**gh**, hi**gh**, ni**gh**t, wei**gh** |
| **l** | cou**l**d, shou**l**d, wou**l**d, ha**l**f, ta**l**k, wa**l**k |
| **t** | lis**t**en, cas**t**le, of**t**en (often), whis**t**le |
| **h** | **h**our, **h**onest, **h**onour, g**h**ost |

**Rule of thumb:** these are learned one by one — when a new word has a silent letter,
I flag it, and it becomes a tracked item like any other.

## Word pairs that sound identical (homophones)

Same sound, different spelling and meaning — pure memory, no rule:
- *son / sun* → both **sun** · *whether / weather* → both **WEH-ther** ·
  *knew / new* → both **new** · *their / there / they're* → all **thair**

## Stress can change the word

Some words are a **noun** or a **verb** depending on where the stress goes:
- *a **RE**cord* (noun) vs *to re**CORD*** (verb)
- *a **PRO**ject* (noun) vs *to pro**JECT*** (verb)
- *deliberate*: **dih-LIB-er-it** (adjective) vs **dih-LIB-er-ayt** (verb)
