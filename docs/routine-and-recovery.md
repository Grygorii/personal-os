# Routine & recovery — your standard clothes

The point: **stop spending fuel on decisions that don't matter.** Pre-decide the day
once, follow it, and save your brain for the work. Your recovery is built into the
routine — because pushing hard (day job + agents + this build + English) only works if
recovery is scheduled, not hoped for.

Grounded in your real data: sleep 6–8h (too variable), "recovery" = shorts (the worst
input), airsoft (good), cycling coming, family. See [[project-body-recovery]].

## The one rule that matters most
**No short-form video as "rest."** It's stimulation, not recovery — it drains the exact
focus you're rebuilding and steals the hours between your 6h and your 8h. Kill this and
half the problem solves itself.

## Daily rhythm (the default day)
| When | Do | Why |
| ---- | -- | --- |
| **Wake — same time daily (~06:45)** | daylight + water first | fixes your body clock; the biggest sleep lever |
| **Deep work — 3–4 blocks of ~90 min** | one thing per block, real break between (walk/stand, *not* phone) | you get ~3–4 true focus hours; the rest is lower gear — accept it |
| **Move** | a walk always; a Zone-2 cycle or airsoft when scheduled | best brain maintenance there is |
| **Family — phone in another room** | presence, not productivity | the place that refills you; don't make it a KPI |
| **Wind-down (from ~21:00)** | no short-form; dim lights; read a real book | protects the 2 hours you keep losing |
| **In bed — same time (~23:00)** | aim 7.5–8h | consistency matters as much as duration |

*(Times are defaults — set your own once in the app, then stop deciding.)*

## Weekly rhythm
- **Cycling 2–3×** — mostly easy (Zone 2: could hold a conversation), ~30–45 min.
- **Airsoft 1×** when you can — real recovery: active, outdoors, social.
- **Family time** — protected blocks, not leftovers.
- **Sunday review** — your System already does a week-in-review; read it.

## Your uniform (pre-decided defaults)
Decide these **once** so they never cost a morning again. Fill your own in the app:
- **Breakfast** — one default you don't rethink.
- **Clothes** — a standard set / rotation.
- **Default movement** — "if unsure: Zone-2 cycle 40 min, or a walk."
- **Evening default** — "reading beats shorts."

## How the app uses this
The **Routine** page shows **Today's 3** (the few things that matter — decided, not
chosen), a daily checklist of the rhythm above (ticks reset each day), and your editable
uniform. Follow it; don't re-litigate it. See [[feedback-minimize-decisions]].
